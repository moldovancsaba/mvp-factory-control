# MVP Factory Playbook

## What is the MVP Factory?

The MVP Factory is our system for rapidly turning ideas into delivered minimum viable products while ensuring every deliverable receives proper executive oversight.

## How We Work

See [MVP Factory Pipeline](./mvp_factory_pipeline.md) for the detailed workflow.

## When We Do It

- Ideas are captured continuously
- Technical prioritization sessions happen weekly
- Executive committee reviews happen every two weeks (or ad-hoc for urgent items)
- Development sprints follow our standard agile process

## Why We Do It This Way

1. **Executive alignment** — Every delivered product has buy-in from company, marketing, and technology leadership
2. **Quality through collaboration** — Product and engineering working together from the start produces better outcomes
3. **Transparency** — Everyone can see where ideas are in the pipeline
4. **Speed with governance** — We move fast but responsibly

## Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|------------------|
| Product Employee | Idea capture, requirements, prioritization, acceptance testing |
| Senior Full Stack Developer | Technical feasibility, architecture, implementation leadership |
| CEO | Strategic alignment, resource decisions |
| CMO | Market positioning, customer value assessment |
| CTO | Technical viability, architecture standards |

## IDEABANK Integration

The Product agent manages an IDEABANK stored in GitHub (moldovancsaba/mvp-factory-control, issues #451-503, #514, #515). Ideas are scored using ICE framework:
- **Impact**: How much value does this deliver?
- **Confidence**: How sure are we this will work?
- **Ease**: How hard is it to build?
