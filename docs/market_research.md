# Market Research: MVP Factory

## Company Overview

**MVP Factory** is a virtual research and development company focused on systematically transforming signals and opportunities into validated, launch-ready digital products.

### Mission
To systematically turn signals, ideas, and emerging technologies into validated digital products and execution-ready ventures.

### Vision
Build a company that functions as a venture production system - repeatedly finding the right idea, defining it properly, validating it quickly, and executing it cleanly.

---

## Target Market Analysis

### Primary Target Audiences

1. **Early-Stage Founders**
   - Validating product ideas before full commitment
   - Limited technical resources
   - Need fast, cost-effective validation

2. **Small Teams**
   - Lack in-house technical capability
   - Want to test market before building team

3. **Serial Entrepreneurs**
   - Need rapid prototyping capability
   - Want to validate multiple ideas simultaneously

### Secondary Audiences

- Agencies looking for rapid prototyping
- Enterprise teams exploring new product concepts
- Consultants needing to validate client ideas
